# Wholesaler trade-account application list — weekend of 2026-09-04

60 UK non-food / baby-product wholesalers to apply to. Compiled by web research
(desk research only — nobody here has been individually confirmed to approve
Brightside; treat "likely approval" as a researched estimate, not a guarantee).

**VAT lesson from Qogita (2026-09-02):** their own help doc said no VAT number
needed below the £90k threshold, but their support rep contradicted that and
said VAT IS required, contradicting their own public page. General UK wholesale
market practice (confirmed across this batch) is that a VAT number is NOT
required — Companies House number / business bank statement / trading proof
is normally enough. But don't fully trust a marketing page over the live
signup form: if a form demands a VAT number with no alternative field, that's
this list's version of the Qogita trap — stop and flag it rather than
guessing an answer.

**Multi-category hubs** (one signup unlocks several categories at once — do
these first, highest time-ROI): Pound Wholesale, MX Wholesale, Harrisons
Direct, EFG Housewares, Something Different Wholesale, Gainsborough Giftware,
Crosswear, Suman Brothers, Clearance King, E-Apollow Wholesale.

**Flagged as harder approval** (established-brand official distributor, likely
wants proof of trading history/turnover before granting terms) — apply last,
lowest expected hit rate: A.B. Gee.

**Legend for "account type":** ONLINE = self-serve web form, usually
same-day/instant. MANUAL = form + human review/callback expected.

## What's still needed before submitting

Every one of these forms will ask for real business details this file
doesn't have: registered/trading address, contact phone, contact email,
trading name / website URL to list, and (for some) years trading or expected
monthly order value. Need that from you before actually filling these in —
see chat.
