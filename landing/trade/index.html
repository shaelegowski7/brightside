About us

Who we are

Brightside Commerce is a small, independent UK retailer based in Bothwell, Scotland. We buy branded products on trade terms and sell them to consumers through our own seller accounts on Amazon UK and eBay UK.

We are a new company and we are upfront about that. What we bring is existing marketplace access, accounts already approved in many brands and categories, and a straightforward way of working: one contact, clear terms, and no surprises about where your products are sold.

Channels

Where we sell

We operate as a third-party seller on the UK's two largest consumer marketplaces: Amazon UK, fulfilled through FBA, and eBay UK, which we fulfil ourselves.

Brand approvals

Approved to list

100+ brands for which Brightside holds approved-seller status on Amazon UK, across homeware, DIY, automotive, health & beauty and toys.

Amazon requires category or brand approval before a seller may list many products. Where that approval is already in place, a new line can go live without an application delay.
